# Figure captions

Generated by `notebooks/06_figures_and_exports.ipynb`; every number in a takeaway comes from the project data.

**Figure 1. Number of SIC 3674 companies per label year.** (fig01_companies_per_year.png)

The sample shrinks from 93 companies in 2015 to 80 in 2025; 106 of 925 company-years are very small companies (revenue below USD 10 million), which are excluded from modelling.

**Figure 2. Revenue, 2015 to 2025 (log scale).** (fig02_revenue.png)

Nvidia's revenue grew from USD 5,010m to 215,938m and overtook Intel's in 2023; Intel fell from a peak of USD 79,024m (2021) to 52,853m, and Wolfspeed shrank from USD 1,633m to 758m. A log scale is used because Nvidia's 2025 revenue is about 285 times Wolfspeed's.

**Figure 3. Net income, 2015 to 2025 (separate scales).** (fig03_net_income.png)

Nvidia was profitable in every year; AMD made losses in 2015, 2016 and profits afterwards; Intel turned to losses in 2024 and 2025 (USD -18,756m in 2024); Wolfspeed lost money in every year from 2015 to 2025, reaching USD -1,609m in 2025.

**Figure 4. Current ratio and quick ratio, with a reference line at 1.** (fig04_liquidity.png)

All four companies kept a current ratio above 1 in every year until Wolfspeed fell to 0.36 in 2025, when its current liabilities rose from USD 665m to 7,090m; Wolfspeed's current ratio was still 4.51 in 2024, so liquidity gave no early warning of its distress.

**Figure 5. Debt-to-equity (log scale) and interest coverage (symmetric log scale).** (fig05_leverage.png)

Intel's interest coverage fell from 32.6 (2021) to 4.7 (2022) and 0.11 (2023) before its 2024 loss, and Wolfspeed's debt-to-equity rose from 0.61 (2022) to 8.05 (2024); AMD's leverage fell from 7.0 in 2016 to 0.22 in 2025. Blank points mark negative equity (AMD 2015, Wolfspeed 2025). Wolfspeed's coverage for 2015 to 2019 uses cash interest paid as a substitute.

**Figure 6. Gross, operating and net margin (percent of revenue).** (fig06_margins.png)

Nvidia's operating margin rose from 15% to 62% (2024); Intel's gross margin fell from 63% to 33% (2024); Wolfspeed's gross margin turned negative in 2025 (-16%), and its operating margin was negative in every year from 2015.

**Figure 7. Return on assets and return on equity (percent).** (fig07_returns.png)

Nvidia's ROE reached 92% (2024) and its ROA 65%; AMD's ROA dropped from 25% (2021) to 2% (2022) as its total assets rose from USD 12,419m to 67,580m with the Xilinx acquisition; ROE is blank where equity was negative (AMD 2015, Wolfspeed 2025).

**Figure 8. Altman Z' score with the distress (1.23) and safe (2.90) cutoffs.** (fig08_zscore.png)

Wolfspeed entered the distress zone in 2020 and Intel in 2023; AMD left it after 2019 (Z' 1.08 to 2.21); Nvidia never fell below 1.23 but was in the grey zone in 2015, 2020, 2021, 2022, contrary to the expectation that it would stay far above 2.90.

**Figure 9. Spearman correlation between the 15 ratios (operating companies, 2015 to 2025).** (fig09_correlation.png)

The strongest correlations are Debt ratio and Debt-to-equity (0.99); ROE and ROA (0.98); ROA and Net margin (0.97); Quick ratio and Current ratio (0.97); ROE and Net margin (0.95). These groups carry overlapping information, which is why logistic regression coefficients for related ratios should not be read one by one; 819 company-years are used.

**Figure 10. Model comparison on the test years (F1, ROC-AUC and recall for the distressed class).** (fig10_model_comparison.png)

The random forest (chosen on validation) reached F1 0.78 and ROC-AUC 0.90, far above the current-ratio rule (F1 0.14), but below the persistence baseline that uses this year's Z' (F1 0.85, ROC-AUC 0.95).

**Figure 11. ROC curves of all models and both baselines on the test years.** (fig11_roc_curves.png)

Random forest, XGBoost and logistic regression lie close together (AUC 0.88 to 0.90) and the decision tree lower (0.85); the persistence baseline lies above all of them (AUC 0.95) and the current-ratio rule below (AUC 0.76).

**Figure 12. Feature importance of the random forest (permutation importance and SHAP).** (fig12_feature_importance.png)

The most important ratios by permutation are Operating margin, Debt-to-equity, Debt ratio; no single feature dominates (the largest SHAP share is 13%), so there is no sign that one variable leaks the label. The error bars (one standard deviation over 30 shuffles) are wide, so the order among the top six is uncertain.

**Figure 13. Walk-forward predicted probability of distress in the following year, 2018 to 2025.** (fig13_case_probabilities.png)

Each year is scored by a model trained only on earlier years. Nvidia stayed below 17%; Wolfspeed was above the threshold for every year from the 2018 data (78%), including a false alarm for 2019, the year before its Z' first fell below 1.23 (2020); Intel's probability rose from 28% (2022, missed) to 85% (2024); the 2025 forecasts for 2026 are Nvidia 9%, AMD 6%, Intel 73%, Wolfspeed 89%.
