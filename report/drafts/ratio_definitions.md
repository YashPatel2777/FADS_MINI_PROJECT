# Ratio definitions

All ratios are calculated from the cleaned statements table (USD millions, one row per company per label year) in `src/ratios.py`. Each formula is written once and applied to every company and year in the same way. Ratios are reported as plain fractions; for example, a net margin of 0.12 means 12 percent.

## Formulas

| Category | Ratio | Formula | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Liquidity | Current ratio | Current assets ÷ Current liabilities | Ability to pay short-term obligations; below 1 is a warning sign |
| Liquidity | Quick ratio | (Current assets − Inventory) ÷ Current liabilities | The same test without relying on selling inventory |
| Liquidity | Cash ratio | Cash and cash equivalents ÷ Current liabilities | The strictest liquidity test |
| Leverage | Debt-to-equity | Total liabilities ÷ Total stockholders' equity | Creditor funding per unit of shareholder funding; all liabilities are used, not only borrowings |
| Leverage | Debt ratio | Total liabilities ÷ Total assets | Share of assets financed by liabilities |
| Leverage | Interest coverage | Operating income ÷ Interest expense | How many times operating profit covers interest; below 1.5 is weak |
| Profitability | Gross margin | Gross profit ÷ Revenue | Pricing power and production cost control |
| Profitability | Operating margin | Operating income ÷ Revenue | Profit from core operations |
| Profitability | Net profit margin | Net income ÷ Revenue | Final profit per dollar of sales |
| Profitability | Return on assets (ROA) | Net income ÷ Total assets | Profit generated by the asset base |
| Profitability | Return on equity (ROE) | Net income ÷ Total stockholders' equity | Return to shareholders |
| Efficiency | Asset turnover | Revenue ÷ Total assets | Sales generated per dollar of assets |
| Growth | Revenue growth | (Revenue this year ÷ Revenue last year) − 1 | Year-on-year change in sales |
| Growth | Net income growth | (Net income this year − Net income last year) ÷ \|Net income last year\| | Year-on-year change in profit; the absolute value keeps the sign meaningful when last year was a loss |
| Size | Log total assets | ln(Total assets in USD millions) | Company size, used as a model input |

## Statement items behind the formulas

- **Equity** is stockholders' equity attributable to the parent company. Where only total equity including non-controlling interests was reported (30 company-years), that figure is used and flagged.
- **Total liabilities** is the reported total. Where a company does not report it (AMD, Intel and Wolfspeed in every year), it is derived as total liabilities and equity − temporary (mezzanine) equity − total equity including non-controlling interests.
- **Net income** is net income attributable to the parent, the bottom line including discontinued operations.
- **Interest expense** is reported interest expense. For the case companies, interest disclosed only in the notes was taken from the same 10-K's XBRL data through the SEC company facts API. Where no interest expense exists, cash interest paid is used as a flagged substitute (Wolfspeed 2015 to 2019). It is set to zero only when the company reports no debt.

## Zero and negative denominators

A ratio is never computed from a zero or negative denominator. It is left blank, and where the reason itself carries information a flag is recorded.

| Situation | Treatment | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current liabilities missing or ≤ 0 | Current, quick and cash ratio blank | The ratio is undefined |
| Equity ≤ 0 | Debt-to-equity and ROE blank; flag `negative_equity` | A negative ratio would wrongly read as low leverage or as a profit/loss reversal; negative equity is itself a distress signal |
| Interest expense = 0 (no debt) | Interest coverage blank; flag `no_interest_expense` | Coverage is unbounded when there is no interest to cover |
| Interest expense not available | Interest coverage blank | Not reported anywhere in the filing data |
| Interest expense is cash paid (proxy) | Coverage computed; flag `coverage_uses_proxy` | Close to the expense, but excludes non-cash interest |
| Revenue ≤ 0 | Gross, operating and net margin blank | Pre-revenue companies have no meaningful margin |
| Last year's revenue ≤ 0 | Revenue growth blank | Growth from zero is undefined |
| Last year's net income = 0 | Net income growth blank | Division by zero |
| Previous label year missing | Both growth ratios blank | Growth is never measured across a gap of two or more years |

Extreme values (mostly from very small shell companies) are not removed here. They are capped at the 1st and 99th percentiles in the modelling phase, with the cutoffs calculated on the training years only.

## Profit and loss summary (case companies)

Reported gross profit and operating income are used as filed. The lines between them are derived so each statement adds up:

- Operating expenses = Gross profit − Operating income
- Other non-operating items = Pre-tax income − (Operating income − Interest expense)
- Discontinued operations and other = Net income − (Pre-tax income − Income tax)
- Profit or loss label: "Profit" if net income ≥ 0, otherwise "Loss".
